# Gate II · Grade

**How much can this thing hurt us?**

Agent: expense-triage

## The worst day

The agent recommends rejecting a legitimate claim with a confident rationale. The reviewer, clearing forty reports before lunch, approves the recommendation without reading the receipt. The employee is out of pocket for five weeks, raises a grievance, and HR asks who decided. The answer is a reviewer who trusted a tool. Cost: one grievance, one reversal, a month of reviewers double-checking everything and the time savings gone.

## Downstream check

Systems that consume the output automatically: none. The recommendation lands in the reviewer's queue and nothing acts on it until a human clicks.
